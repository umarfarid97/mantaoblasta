# Product

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Blasta Empire presents Malaysian golden fried mantao catering packages and helps visitors enquire about events through the existing WhatsApp booking flow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React, TypeScript and Vite. Keep the original website available. The separate reference-inspired page uses existing products, prices, imagery and enquiry flow, confirmed by the user. Package and sauce data remain in src/data/packagesData.ts.

## Brand Commitments

Use Blasta branding and existing food photographs. The user requested a separate design based on the supplied cream, sage green and orange café reference.
